The six levels

The campaign follows the epic in order. Each level adds one new idea.

#LevelWhat you defendWhat makes it differentBosses

4Rama SetuThe vanaras building the bridgeThe map grows each wave as the bridge extendsSea and sky raiders
5The Siege of LankaThe army's campUnseen night raids, then holding out for the Sanjeevani herbIndrajit, Kumbhakarna
6RavanaRama himself, in the final duelTen heads that regrow until you earn the BrahmastraRavana
