RAMAYANA

The Six Nights of Siddhashrama

Sage Vishwamitra has begun a yajna that must burn for six nights. The rakshasas of the forest mean to defile it. You are Rama. With Lakshmana at your side, guard the sacred fire until the sixth dawn.
